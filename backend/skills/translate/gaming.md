STYLE: Gaming stream / esports commentary / let's play. Output Vietnamese
must match streamer energy — fast, casual, hype, with proper game jargon.

## Pronouns
- **mình / các bạn** — friendly streamer to viewers
- **tao / mày** — close-friend stream, raid party banter (chỉ nếu tone phù hợp)
- **anh em** — addressing the audience as a group

## KEEP English gaming jargon — DO NOT translate
Build, item, boss, raid, dungeon, PvP, PvE, MMO, GG, AFK, AFK farm,
nerf, buff, meta, tank, DPS, support, healer, jungle, lane, top/mid/bot,
ult, combo, cooldown, CD, farm, last hit, gank, ward, vision, peel,
tower dive, baron, dragon, herald, KDA, MVP, clutch, ace, pentakill,
respawn, dispel, root, stun, slow, silence, knockup, true damage,
penetration, lifesteal, omnivamp, crit, evasion, dodge.

## Vietnamese gamer slang (dùng tự nhiên)
- **chuối** — noob, gà
- **gánh team** — carry
- **cân team** — solo carry
- **cày** — farm / grind
- **sấp mặt** — get destroyed
- **ngon** — good play / good item
- **cọp** — try-hard / serious
- **trẻ trâu** — toxic kid
- **cắm** — camp
- **mọp** — hide / stealth
- **tủ** — main champion / main signature
- **đu càng** — leech, feed off teammates
- **ngất** — die
- **úp sọt** — gank / ambush
- **bay màu** — fall / die
- **trừ tướng** — pick a champion (esports cast)

## Tone
- Hype, fast, casual
- Allow exclamations: "ôi", "trời ơi", "chết rồi", "đỉnh quá"
- Reaction first-person — streamer is reacting in real time
- Match the energy: combat moment = hyper, lobby moment = chill

## Avoid
- Dịch jargon ra Việt ("ulti" thành "kỹ năng tối thượng" — NO)
- Quá formal — không ai stream mà nói như đọc tin tức
- Dịch tên tướng / item theo nghĩa đen — giữ tên gốc
