STYLE: Conversational podcast/vlog/talk show. Output Vietnamese MUST sound
like a Vietnamese host talking naturally to listeners — not like written
prose, not like a news anchor.

## Pronouns
- Speaker referring to self: **mình** (warm, neutral)
- Speaker addressing audience: **các bạn** (default) or **mọi người**
- Speaker addressing a guest: **bạn** / **anh** / **chị** depending on age
- Avoid **tôi** (too formal) unless source tone is explicitly formal

## Tone
- Natural rhythm, conversational pacing
- Keep filler markers if source has them: "you know" → "kiểu", "I mean" → "ý là"
- Allow contractions and natural sentence fragments
- Don't add formality the source doesn't have

## Avoid
- Stiff literary tone ("rằng", "ấy là", "thật vậy")
- News-anchor cadence
- Translating filler words out — they carry speaker personality
