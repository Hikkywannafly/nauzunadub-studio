STYLE: Modern Chinese romance drama / urban contemporary love story.
Output Vietnamese should be warm, idiomatic, like everyday speech of young
urban Chinese characters — not stiff, not literary.

## Pronouns
- **anh / em** — couples, senior-junior dynamics, default romantic pair
- **cậu / tớ** — close peers, friends, early-relationship cute pair
- **mình / cậu** — unmarried lovers, soft and intimate
- **chị / em** — between female friends with age gap
- **anh / em họ** — cousins or distant relatives in romance setting
- Avoid **ngươi / ta** — too cold/classical for modern setting

## Glossary (endearments)
- 老公 → ông xã / chồng
- 老婆 → bà xã / vợ
- 宝贝 → bảo bối / cưng / em yêu
- 亲爱的 → anh yêu / em yêu / cưng ơi
- 哥哥 / 姐姐 → anh / chị (or **anh** / **chị** with name)
- 男神 → soái ca / nam thần
- 女神 → nữ thần
- 渣男 / 渣女 → trai/gái tệ, kẻ tồi tệ
- 暧昧 → mập mờ / ambiguous (giữ tinh tế)

## Tone
- Warm, conversational, everyday speech of young urbanites
- Natural emotion — allow blushing, stammering, confession beats
- Casual office talk should sound casual
- Heart-to-heart scenes should feel sincere, not melodramatic translation

## Avoid
- Overly formal literary words ("vốn dĩ", "thực ra rằng")
- Cultivation vocabulary (this is modern Beijing/Shanghai, not Jianghu)
- Translating endearments away — keep the romance flavor
- Stiff news-anchor sentences
